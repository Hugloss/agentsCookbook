One clear plan, checked from every angle.

A visual walkthrough for non-technical stakeholders. The demo shows how one lead planning agent creates the final answer while specialist reviewers reduce mistakes before implementation.

Step 1 of 5: Request

Request

The stakeholder asks for a clear business outcome.

Scenario: contact page request

What happens

The request is captured as a concrete goal: add a simple contact page with a form, opening hours, and a map link.

What this prevents

The team does not jump straight into code without agreeing what the user asked for.

Sample output

"Add a simple contact page with a form, opening hours, and a map link."

Deep review loop

Step 3 is where the plan gets sharper. Reviewers are asked to challenge the draft, not politely agree with it. Consensus means the master plan adopts the strongest grounded feedback, not whatever most models said.

Master Drafts plan

The lead planner creates the first working plan from the user request and repo context.

->
Reviewer A Challenges assumptions

One LLM tries to find false assumptions, weak facts, missing validation, or a simpler path.

->
Master Updates plan

The lead planner accepts useful feedback and rejects anything unsupported or out of scope.

->
Reviewer B Reviews again

A second LLM checks the updated plan from another angle and looks for remaining flaws.

->
Master Consensus-improves

The lead planner compares both reviews and produces a sharper plan with grounded fixes.

Did we assume a form component exists without checking?
Did we miss mobile layout or form error states?
Did we add unrequested CRM, email, or analytics work?

Gate gauntlet

Master owns the plan the whole time. Each gate reports findings back to Master, Master updates the canonical plan, and only then does the next quality gate run.

Master Holds improved plan

ping-pong-plan starts Step 4 with the improved plan from Step 3 and remains the only owner.

Validation Can we prove it works?

plan-validation-designer checks tests, manual checks, and pass/fail acceptance criteria.

Master update Before red-team

Master accepts grounded validation fixes and updates the plan before the next gate.

Risk What could go wrong?

plan-red-team-gate looks for blockers, hidden ambiguity, weak validation, and scope creep.

Master update Before simulation

Master fixes real risk findings and rejects unsupported or out-of-scope feedback.

Feasibility Can an engineer follow it?

plan-implementation-simulator dry-runs the plan and flags unclear steps or bad sequencing.

Master update Before fact audit

Master clarifies file targets, sequencing, and handoff wording before the next quality gate.

Facts Is it grounded?

plan-fact-auditor checks that files, commands, and repo claims are real or labeled as assumptions.

Master update Before contract check

Master labels uncertainty and removes unsupported claims before final quality checking.

Final rules Is the answer clean?

plan-contract-checker verifies structure, scope, validation, rollback, and no leaked internal notes.

Master Ends with final plan

Master applies required final fixes and returns the gate-cleared plan to the user.

Can the acceptance criteria be answered yes or no?
Would an engineer know which area to inspect first?
Are unknown repo details marked as assumptions instead of facts?

All agents at a glance

Friendly names come first for the presentation. Technical IDs are included for people who want to connect the demo back to the OpenCode config.

Lead planner ping-pong-plan

Owns the final plan and chooses which feedback to use.

Second opinion plan-improver-model2

Looks for missing details or a better path.

Third opinion plan-improver-model3

Reviews the plan from another model.

Validation designer plan-validation-designer

Defines how to prove the work is complete.

Risk reviewer plan-red-team-gate

Finds blockers, hidden risks, and scope creep.

Implementation dry run plan-implementation-simulator

Checks whether an engineer can follow the plan.

Fact checker plan-fact-auditor

Checks that claims are grounded in repo facts.

Final quality checker plan-contract-checker

Confirms the final answer follows the rules.

What the engineer receives

The final answer is a single implementation plan, not a pile of internal notes.
